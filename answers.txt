1) Some pros of Agile dvelopment are that it allows for dynamic changes of the project requirements as well as
that it allows for input from the stakeholders as development happens. Some cons of Agile development are that
there is less of a steady and well defined development process and it is more time consuming to constantly review
progress and go through the revisions that are a part of Agile development.

2) The product owner manages the backlog, coordinates between the business and the team, and decides when the
product should be completed. The scrum master helps to facilitate the processes involved in the scrum, including
meetings, planning, updates to goals, and development review.

3) The 3 types of meetings that happen during a sprint cycle are sprint planning, sprint review, and standup
meetings. Sprint planning meetings discuss the goals for the upcoming sprint as well as what the team will do to
achieve those goals. Sprint reviews are where completed work is discussed and demonstrated, as well as where
stakeholder input is gathered to better mvoe forward in the development process. Standup meetings are where the
development team discusses what each team member is planning to do for the day, as well as what they hve already
completed and any anticipated problems that they will have to deal with.
